# Bug (OPEN): dense composed stops revert whole to the "shotgun" stitch because the faithfulness gate rejects faithful sentences and revert is all-or-nothing

**Filed:** 2026-07-13 · **Surface:** tour compose (AI voice) · **Severity:** High (the user-facing symptom in the Le Meurice report is NOT closed) · **Status:** OPEN — root cause proven live, fix not yet built. This is the real remaining work after the under-citation sub-fix (commit `f97e104`, companion doc `2026-07-13-compose-fusion-fallback.md`).

## Relationship to the committed sub-fix
The under-citation fix (verify.py strict-first + per-stop-union fallback, `_populate_also_cites`, `undercited_fusions` metric) is **correct and necessary** — it makes a correctly-cited cross-beat fusion survive, and in the live probe below the von Choltitz fusion (the originally-reported sentence) now PASSES. But it does **not** close the Le Meurice bug, because the stop reverts for a different, dominant reason proven below.

## Root cause (proven with a live probe, 2026-07-13)
Composed the real Le Meurice stop (config below) and tested every sentence that failed strict entailment against the union of ALL three of the stop's assigned anchor beats (`c9c90a74` English-corridor, `c3d4a78a` Dickens+von Choltitz, `85ebe707` Orwell+High-Command):

| Failing composed sentence | Cited beats | Entailed by FULL stop union? |
|---|---|---|
| "…the German High Command…took up residence…including General von Choltitz…" | both (85ebe707 + c3d4a78a) | **YES** — handled by the sub-fix |
| "It was also the world a penniless George Orwell served, worked to the bone as a plongeur…" | 85ebe707 | **NO** |
| "When the Tuileries was still the seat of power, the ladies of the Napoleonic nobility…" (long opener) | c9c90a74 + c3d4a78a | **NO** |

Two facts this proves:
1. **The Haiku faithfulness checker (`HaikuFaithfulnessChecker.entails`, verify.py) returns NO on faithful sentences.** The Orwell sentence's facts are ENTIRELY inside its cited beat `85ebe707` (the beat's own `script_body` contains "the world a penniless George Orwell served … worked to the bone as a plongeur, 'the slave of the slave'"), yet Haiku rejects the composed rephrase. Long, dense, multi-clause fusions (the opener) also fail even against the full union. These are false-negatives on faithful composition — not fabrications, not under-citation.
2. **Revert is all-or-nothing.** In `compose_script_per_chapter` (compose.py ~660-686), when `drop_failing_sentences` removes a failing sentence and that leaves a claim uncovered, `repair_composed` (compose_gate.py:93) reverts the ENTIRE stop to the grounded stitch. So a single unrescued sentence discards the whole (mostly-good) composed stop — reproducing the scattered "shotgun of factoids".

Net: dense multi-beat stops reliably generate ≥1 sentence Haiku won't pass (rephrase or long fusion), and one is enough to revert the whole stop. The specific failing sentences vary run to run (Haiku is temp=0 deterministic per call, but the composer's output is stochastic); the pattern is stable.

## Proposed fix (the real lever — larger scope; touches the repair path + gate)
1. **Granular per-sentence repair instead of whole-stop revert.** When a composed beat sentence fails, replace ONLY that sentence with its corresponding grounded STITCHED sentence(s) for the same beat (preserving coverage), and KEEP every passing composed sentence. Le Meurice would then ship mostly-composed (von Choltitz fused) with only the Haiku-rejected sentence grounded — the symptom is gone. This is a redesign of `drop_failing_sentences`/`repair_composed` so the fallback granularity is per-sentence-to-grounded, never per-stop-to-stitch.
2. **Investigate the Haiku entailment false-negative rate** on faithful rephrasings/long sentences (verify.py `_ENTAILMENT_PROMPT`). Options to weigh: allow a composed sentence that is a faithful rephrase of a single beat's `script_body` (verbatim-ish shortcut is already there for exact matches — extend to near-matches?), split long fused sentences before entailment, or a more lenient-but-still-safe entailment prompt. DANGER: loosening entailment risks the fabrication guard the skeptics protect — any change here needs the full adversarial panel + the fabrication negative-fixtures.

## Attempt 1 of the safe half — BUILT, REVIEWED, STOPPED (2026-07-13, `/team` Tier 2)
A per-sentence grounded-restore rung (`restore_grounded_for_uncovered` in compose_gate.py, wired as rung 2 of `compose_script_per_chapter`'s repair ladder between drop and whole-stop revert) was built and run through the full team. It passed the hermetic undo-test (mutation-verified load-bearing), `make lint`, and the bar (zero new failures vs baseline), and a live gold-proof showed rung 2 firing on a real Haiku ceiling hit. **But the skeptic panel + judge STOPPED the commit — it is NOT a correct fix**, for a reason that IS the ticket's own warning ("do NOT close on fixtures alone"):

- **It silently loses a real fact on the flagship stop.** Beat `85ebe707` has `key_claims=None`, so `verify_claim_coverage` is blind to its prose. `split_sentences` packs the Orwell rat-hole/kitchens/scullery QUOTE block AND the trailing sentence "the German High Command of the Paris garrison took up residence at the lavish Meurice" into ONE compound stitch sentence. Live (`gold_proof_v2.log`): the composer voiced High Command in a fused sentence, Haiku rejected it, it was dropped; restore skipped the grounded compound because `skip_if_voiced` saw its kitchens/scullery siblings already voiced by surviving composed sentences (overlap ≥ COVERAGE_MATCH_MIN) → "High Command took up residence" is ABSENT from the shipped stop. The OLD whole-stop revert reverts to the stitch, which ships that compound verbatim → the fact survives. So the attempt trades "complete-but-shotgun" for "readable-but-lossy" — **a regression vs HEAD on the one input we have live proof for**, and a violation of never-lose-a-fact.

- **Root cause is upstream of the repair path:** (a) `key_claims=None` prose-only beats are invisible to coverage; (b) `split_sentences` over-groups a quote block with a following independent sentence into one compound unit, so per-sentence restore cannot extract the single lost fact without either duplicating the whole quote block or a granularity fix.

- **Options for the real fix (a scope decision — none is "safe half"):** (1) make `restore`'s `skip_if_voiced` per-FACT not per-sentence (restore a compound when it carries ANY unvoiced fact; re-opens the bounded double-voicing tradeoff the skeptics flagged, needs its own live proof + panel); (2) backfill `key_claims` on the prose-only beats (data; still restores the whole compound → kitchens duplicated); (3) split the compound in `split_sentences` (cleanest result, but touches every quoted beat in BOTH cities — Tier 3 regression risk). A "completeness net that whole-reverts on incompleteness" was rejected: it guarantees no loss but reverts Le Meurice to the shotgun, so it does not close this ticket.

- **Where the work is:** shelved on branch `wip/compose-per-sentence-restore` (code + tests + `tools/diagnose_compose_stop_revert_fix.py`), with a red-first `xfail` guard `test_restore_grounded_preserves_untracked_prose_fused_in_a_compound_stitch_sentence` (tests/test_tour_recompose.py) that reproduces the REAL compound structure and stays red until the compound case is genuinely fixed. Ticket remains **OPEN**.
