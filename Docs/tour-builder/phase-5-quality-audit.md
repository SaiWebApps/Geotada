# Phase 5 Quality Audit — Tour-Builder Output Review

**Audit date:** 2026-04-29
**Auditor:** main coordination chat (read all 3 novel-tour markdown files end-to-end)
**Trigger:** user reported tours felt below the empirical-walks quality bar
**Verdict:** Phase 5 is NOT launch-ready. Runtime works as plumbing (validation passes, costs bounded, generation <30s). OUTPUT QUALITY is well below the empirical bar for off-corridor starting points.

---

## What was audited

Three novel tours generated by the Phase 5 multi-tour smoke test:

- `data/paris/tours/sacre-coeur-basilica-90min-60d781.md` — Tour 3, Montmartre area, 90min round-trip
- `data/paris/tours/place-de-la-concorde-180min-6bae6e.md` — Tour 4, Champs-Élysées area, 180min one-way
- `data/paris/tours/pantheon-120min-51a0cf.md` — Tour 5, Latin Quarter, 120min round-trip

(Tours 1 and 2 were calibration sanity checks — they reproduced the empirical Place des Vosges and Île de la Cité walks, which are known-good. The audit focuses on the genuinely-novel outputs.)

> **Historical record.** The three tour files above were artifacts local to the audit machine (`data/*/tours/` is machine-local, never committed); they are cited as the evidence this audit read, not as files a reader can open. Regenerate equivalents with `make tour-build`.

---

## Six concrete failures, ranked by impact

### 1. Audio fill rate is catastrophic

| Tour | Audio time | Walk time | Fill rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 min | 90 min | 5.5% |
| 4 | 9 min | 180 min | 5% |
| 5 | 13 min | 120 min | 10.8% |

The empirical walks ran 70–80% audio fill. **Phase 5 tours give the user 80+ minutes of silence on a 90-minute walk.** Not a tour — a quiet stroll with occasional voice cues.

### 2. Cold opens are uniformly robotic

Every tour opens with the same three sentences:

```
Settle in. [GLUE_PACING]
You're standing at [POI Name]. [SYNTHESIZED_OPENER]
Look around. Take it in. [GLUE_STAGING]
```

Compare to PdV's actual opener: *"Find a bench in the garden, near the children's play area, so you can watch the children play à la française — and despite the mix of Hebrew, Yiddish, and Arabic you'll hear..."*

The synthesized opener is announcing the POI name, not staging the listener. It doesn't even use the available `physical_cues` data. The placeholder template shipped as if it were the final implementation.

### 3. Transit beats are geographically broken when reused

The corpus's transit beats encode a one-way direction (where the original guidebook walked you). Reusing them in arbitrary routes produces gibberish.

Tour 3 stop 3 (Saint-Pierre) opens: *"Leave the Sacré-Cœur, turn right into rue Azaïs..."* — but the user hasn't been to Sacré-Cœur yet (it's stop 4).

Tour 4 stop 3 (Pont Alexandre III) opens: *"Starting at Invalides Metro station, walk to Pont Alexandre III..."* — but the user is arriving from Pont de la Concorde.

Tour 4 stop 2 says: *"Once across the bridge you come to the statue of Churchill. Turn right along cours la Reine stopping at Pont de la Concorde to admire the spectacular views before crossing back over to the Left Bank."* — describes a route that bears no relation to the actual generated walk.

### 4. Empty stops in the route

Tour 4 stop 4 is **Petit Palais with 0 beats**. The script for it is literally just "Walk to the next stop." Selection let a 0-beat POI through as an anchor. This is a Phase 2/4 selection bug, not a corpus issue.

### 5. B8-lite missed obvious duplicates

Tour 5 has two near-identical accounts of the Pantheon's founding (beats `ee115ca8` and `142060a7`): both tell Louis XV → St Geneviève → 1744 vow → Soufflot → Revolution conversion. The user hears the same story twice, in different words.

Either the entity/subject_tag overlap fell below 0.8 (extraction quality issue: subject_tag is empty too often to be reliable) or the threshold needs lowering. Both should be investigated.

### 6. Sentence segmentation breaks proper names

Tour 3 lines 71–73:

```
The artist who painted the sign was called A. [BEAT:5d99aca6]
Gill. [BEAT:5d99aca6]
```

"A. Gill" got split mid-name on the period. Same beat ID twice. Looks ridiculous in audio.

## Structural absences

- **No anchor essays.** The empirical walks open each tier-5 stop with a 200–400-word anchor (1612 inauguration at PdV; "great ship moored by huge flying buttresses" at Notre-Dame). Phase 5 tours don't produce these — beats sequence by source order at the stop.
- **No narrative throughline.** Tours 3-5 are POI lists with random beats attached. No theme even attempted.
- **Closings are abrupt.** Most close mid-thought (e.g., Tour 4 ends mid-Tour-de-France-jersey-rules). The closing glue fires on the last beat regardless of where it lands.

---

## Diagnosis

Phase 4's 100% golden overlap on PdV/Île was real — but it was **overfitting to the two empirical corridors.** Off-corridor tours expose the algorithm's weak points.

The proximate root causes:

1. **Transit reuse logic is fundamentally flawed.** Pre-existing transit beats are direction-specific. They should be used ONLY when the route direction matches the beat's source direction, OR they should be lightly rewritten as glue (which the current Haiku stitcher doesn't do — it only writes nav glue when no transit beat exists, even if the existing one is wrong-direction).

2. **Cold-open synthesizer is a stub.** "You're standing at X. Look around. Take it in." is placeholder text that shipped as if it were the final implementation.

3. **Audio fill is not a gate.** Phase 4 calibrated to beat-overlap percentage. It never asked "does the resulting tour have enough content for the duration?"

4. **Zero-beat POI exclusion missing.** Selection lets 0-beat POIs through as anchors when they're routing-favourable.

5. **B8-lite tuning is too loose.** The Genevieve duplication suggests the threshold needs adjustment OR claim canonicalization needs to be smarter.

The deeper diagnosis (surfaced by user pushback): the failure mode is NOT per-Area thinness. It's **per-start-point density** — given a starting GPS point, is there enough beat content within walking radius to support the requested duration?

This is fundamentally different from per-Area gating. Tours cross Area boundaries naturally; what matters is the reachable beat pool from the start point at the requested duration, regardless of which Area(s) it intersects.

## Re-diagnosis through the per-start-point lens

| Tour | Real root cause |
|---|---|
| 3 (Sacré-Cœur 90min round-trip) | Round-trip radius too small from a hilltop. 550m round-trip radius pulls in very few beats. Solution: refuse round-trip from sparse starting points; offer one-way descending into Pigalle/9e (denser corpus). |
| 4 (Concorde 180min one-way) | Petit Palais 0-beat selection — a SELECTION bug. The corridor itself is dense. Fix: zero-beat-POI exclusion. |
| 5 (Pantheon 120min round-trip) | Genuinely YELLOW: 13min audio for 120min walk = 11% fill. Round-trip from Pantheon is tight. Should have offered one-way through Latin Quarter into Île de la Cité. |

## Phase 6 design — density gating

For a (start_lat, start_lng, duration_min, round_trip) input:

```
walk_radius_m = (duration_min × 0.83 × 0.4 × 3000 ÷ 1.35) ÷ 60
if round_trip: walk_radius_m /= 2

reachable_pois  = POIs within walk_radius AND poi_role IN ['stop','setting']
reachable_beats = active beats at reachable POIs (excluding 0-beat POIs)
audio_capacity_s = SUM(est_spoken_seconds for reachable_beats)
target_audio_s   = duration_min × 0.83 × 0.6 × 60
fill_ratio       = audio_capacity_s / target_audio_s

anchor_candidates = reachable POIs with tier ≥ 3 AND beat_count ≥ 3
cluster_compactness = mean_pairwise_distance(anchor_candidates) / walk_radius

GREEN  if fill_ratio ≥ 1.0 AND len(anchor_candidates) ≥ 4 AND cluster_compactness ≤ 0.6
YELLOW if 0.5 ≤ fill_ratio < 1.0 OR (anchor_candidates ≥ 3 AND cluster_compactness ≤ 0.7)
RED    otherwise
```

Status drives runtime behavior:
- **GREEN** — generate as normal
- **YELLOW** — generate but warn explicitly: "Tour ran at X% fill, feels thin. Consider Y-min duration for richer content."
- **RED** — refuse to generate. Surface alternatives: shorter duration, one-way that reaches denser territory, different starting area.

Plus diagnostic: `scripts/tourability_map.py` pre-computes status across a 100m grid covering Paris. Output is a heatmap showing GREEN/YELLOW/RED zones per duration bucket — useful for product UX (showing users where rich tours are available) and for launch-readiness (what fraction of central Paris is GREEN?).

## Concrete fixes to ship in Phase 6

1. `src/tour/density.py` — implement the formula as a reusable utility
2. `scripts/tourability_map.py` — pre-compute density across Paris grid; output heatmap diagnostic
3. `selection.py` — add zero-beat-POI exclusion + density gate at top of pipeline
4. `tour-build` skill — handle TourabilityRefused (RED), warn on YELLOW, generate normally on GREEN
5. Re-run all 5 Phase 5 tours under new gates; verify Tours 3-5 either improve or are honestly refused with actionable alternatives

Expected outcomes:
- Tour 1 (PdV): GREEN, no change
- Tour 2 (Île): GREEN, no change
- Tour 3 (Sacré-Cœur round-trip): YELLOW or RED → recommends one-way descending
- Tour 4 (Concorde): GREEN with Petit Palais excluded → quality lifts substantially
- Tour 5 (Pantheon round-trip): YELLOW → warns or recommends one-way

## What this audit does NOT cover

- Cold-open synthesizer fix (separate sub-task; backlog item 4 — stop_orientation gap-fill via extraction is the proper fix)
- Transit beat direction-awareness (separate sub-task; needs route-geometry-aware beat reuse logic)
- B8-lite recalibration (separate sub-task)
- Anchor essay structure enforcement (separate sub-task)
- Sentence segmentation handling for initials in proper names (regex fix)

These are real issues worth their own phases or backlog tickets but are NOT what density gating addresses. Density gating handles the "thin tour shipped at all" failure; cold-open quality handles "tour shipped is generic-sounding"; transit fixes handle "tour is geographically incoherent." Each needs its own scoped fix.

---

## Strategic implication for launch

Phase 6 (density gates) is the minimum viable improvement to make /tour-build honest. Most of central Paris should pass GREEN at typical durations once the gates are in place — the corpus is denser than per-Area framing suggests. Specific failure modes (round-trip from sparse hilltops, lens combos that filter too aggressively, individual 0-beat POIs) get caught and either refused or warned.

Beyond Phase 6, the cold-open + transit + B8-lite + anchor-essay issues are quality-of-output rather than ship-or-don't-ship gates. They can ship as known gradients in Phase 6 markdown footers, with extraction and refinement filling them in over time.

The push-divergence reconciliation, polygon hygiene, book_slug backfill, and stop_orientation extraction gap-fill remain in the post-launch backlog and gate before public visibility, not before /tour-build internal usability.
