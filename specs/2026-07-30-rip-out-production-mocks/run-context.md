## Escalation for the owner (answer before execution)

"Rip out ALL mocks" has two readings. The plan implements (b):

- **(a) Delete the mock classes entirely.** Breaks the $0 hermetic bar — the
  audio, onboarding and compose suites all depend on free doubles. Would
  force `make test` to spend real money on every run, contradicting
  standing rules.
- **(b) Remove every path by which a fake can be selected, resolved or
  served on a human-facing surface**, while the doubles remain reachable
  only from inside a pytest interpreter. Nothing a human touches can
  produce fake output; the bar stays free.

Recommendation: **(b)**. It is what makes the workbench honest, which is the
stated concern, and (a) trades that for a bill on every test run with no
added safety. If the owner wants (a), it becomes a much larger piece of
work: every suite that depends on a double needs a recorded-fixture or
live-credential replacement first, and that should be its own ledger.
