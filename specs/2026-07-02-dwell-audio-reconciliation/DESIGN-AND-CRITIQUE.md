# Dwell / voiced-audio reconciliation — design + adversarial critique (DEFERRED)

**Status: DEFERRED by decision 2026-07-02.** The adversarial critique found a
FATAL direction error in the recommended step (honest per-stop accounting is
pointwise >= the current tier-dwell proxy, so it provably SHRINKS routes and
opens an unguarded greedy-break 1-stop collapse — the failure class the
endpoint-pull guard just closed), it stacks route-shrinkage on the same-day
pace pin, and the golden gap closes via R1 (beat emission), which pulls the
opposite direction. The open product decisions it enumerates (duration-promise
surfaces on mobile, golden interplay ordering, a min-stop floor for the greedy
break) need human calls before any implementation. Design and critique are
preserved verbatim below so the future session starts from the corrected
picture, not from scratch.

## Design (as produced — read WITH the critique's corrections)

DESIGN (no code changed): reconcile selection's tier-constant dwell time model with generation's voiced-audio model. Verified inventory: selection budgets/consumes audio as DWELL_SECONDS_BY_TIER (300s for tier 4/5) at selection.py:1058,1061,1370,1422 while generation sums real per-beat spoken seconds (word_count@150wpm fallback) at generation.py:824-851; beat_select's spatial strategies are uncapped (Rue Cler: 39 beats, 19 addresses -> 20-beat plan = 2,042 words ~= 817s voiced vs 300s budgeted; PdV fixture: 18 unique beats = 2,286 words ~= 914s at a single stop); Route.audio_budget_seconds (contract.py:231, routing.py:250) has zero consumers and even disagrees with the target_audio_seconds definition selection uses; every ETA/duration surface (options.py:107-109,156; trips.py:522; crud/trips.py:45,67,337; audit.py:100-107) reports the dwell fiction while /trips/preview's total_audio_min (trips.py:580) is already voiced-truth — self-contradictory within one response. Three candidate designs analyzed (A: audio-true greedy accounting; B: audio-derived dwell everywhere; C: two-pass select->plan->re-budget). Recommendation: a staged hybrid of A+B — (1) extract one shared beat-seconds function, (2) make SelectedPOI.dwell_seconds = max(tier floor, planned voiced audio) so all surfaces turn honest with ZERO route-shape change, (3) switch selection's consumed-audio to the same planned-audio function (floored at tier dwell), verified against goldens/grade Valhalla-up, (4) resolve the dead Route.audio_budget_seconds field. Explicitly NOT a naive per-stop beat cap — that works against the golden aspiration (GOLDEN-GAP-DIAGNOSTIC.md attributes the 53.2%/66.7% gap to too FEW beats); PdV's 1-stop 22-beat domination is preserved by design because beat emission is untouched and the honest accounting only reinforces PdV-first selection. Four decisions need a human: duration-promise semantics (keep 60/40 split, change only the audio currency), dwell floor semantics (silence/look-around time), whether one incidental POI may eat unbounded share of a multi-stop tour's audio (Rue Cler ate 45%), and est_spoken_seconds backfill vs deprecation (live corpus has it ~unpopulated: Rue Cler's 39 beats sum to 24s).

### INVENTORY 1 — Selection's audio currency is a per-tier constant; generation's is per-beat voiced seconds

Selection: DWELL_SECONDS_BY_TIER (routing.py:48-54; tier 5/4=300s, 3=150s, 2=60s, 1=0s) via compute_dwell_seconds (routing.py:125-126), consumed by the greedy at selection.py:1058 with break `consumed_audio >= audio_budget` at :1061 (audio_budget = target_audio_seconds(duration_min), selection.py:999 = duration*0.83*0.6*60, routing.py:98-99), and by the fill pass at selection.py:1370 and :1422 (floor = 0.8*budget, FILL_PASS_AUDIO_FLOOR_FRAC selection.py:300). Generation: _sum_audio (generation.py:824-851) sums per-beat est_spoken_seconds, falling back to word_count/150wpm (:848-849), + 4s per glue/vignette sentence. Same tour, two different clocks. The 150wpm rate itself is consistent codebase-wide: generation.py:849, reflection.py:32 (_WORDS_PER_MINUTE=150), density.py:274-276 (word_count/2.5 = 150wpm).

### INVENTORY 2 — beat_select spatial strategies are uncapped; live proof: Rue Cler 20 beats ~= 817s voiced vs 300s budgeted

choose_ordering_strategy (beat_select.py:104-121) picks trigger_address at >=5 distinct addresses; _order_by_trigger_address (:206-221) emits ONE beat per distinct address + one no-addr closer with NO count or seconds cap; _order_by_sub_location (:172-203) likewise uncapped. Only the narrative_function fallback caps (tier-3 -> PAUSE_BEATS_MAX=3 at :248-249, tier-1/2 -> 1 at :250-252, tier-5/4 -> DEFAULT_FLAT_MAX=8 at :253-255). Verified live (dev Neo4j 7687): Rue Cler tier 5, 39 active NarrativeBeats, 19 distinct trigger_addresses, 18 sub_locations -> trigger_address strategy, 20-bucket plan; max-word winner per bucket sums 2,042 words ~= 817s ~= 13.6 min voiced, vs the 300s the selection model books for the stop. On the 2026-07-02 collapse tour (60-min one-way, budget: err_short 2,988s, walk 1,195s, audio target 1,793s) that single incidental stop is ~45% of the total tour promise, invisible to selection.

### INVENTORY 3 — Route.audio_budget_seconds is produced, never consumed, and contradicts selection's own budget definition

Field declared at contract.py:231, computed once at routing.py:250 as `max(0, err_short_total_seconds(duration) - total_walk_seconds)` (residual-after-walk model). ### INVENTORY 5 — est_spoken_seconds is effectively unpopulated in the live corpus; word_count fallback is the real currency

Verified live: Rue Cler's 39 active beats sum est_spoken_seconds = 24s total (i.e., ~all zero). The loader (selection.py:401 Cypher, :485-486) coerces null->0 and computes BeatRef.word_count from script_body at load time, so every design below must treat word_count@150wpm as the primary signal with est_spoken_seconds as an optional override — which is exactly what generation.py:846-849, reflection.py:57-60, and density.py:274-276 already do, in three separate private copies. ### INVENTORY 6 — the mismatch runs BOTH directions and the golden gap forbids the naive fix

Direction 1 (under-count): beat-rich stops (Rue Cler 817s, PdV ~914s) booked at 300s -> tours silently over-deliver audio vs the duration promise, and the greedy keeps seating stops it has no time to voice. Direction 2 (over-count): the golden gap (53.2%/66.7% vs 0.90) is attributed to beat_select emitting too FEW beats — 22 of 28 missed beats die to single-winner slots/caps (diagnostic §2, category e), and §5's feasibility caveat explicitly says a relaxation must be audio-aware ('emit extra closers only while the POI's dwell allowance has headroom'), not a blanket cap. Therefore a naive per-stop beat/seconds cap is ruled out: it would cap PdV's mandated 22-beat circumnavigation (fixture note 2 requires trigger_address strategy) and push both goldens FURTHER from 0.90. Also unaccounted anywhere in selection: vignette one-liners (flat 4s each, generation.py:830) — negligible, noted for completeness.

### INVENTORY 7 — density gate already uses real beat-seconds while the greedy uses dwell constants (intra-selection inconsistency)

density.py:184-201 computes corridor audio_capacity_s from _beat_spoken_seconds (est_spoken_seconds else word_count/2.5, :274-276) and gates GREEN/YELLOW/RED on fill_ratio vs target_audio_s — so the corridor-level feasibility check is voiced-audio-true, but the greedy that then runs inside a GREEN corridor books the same beats at 300s/stop (selection.py:1058). A corridor can be GREEN because one POI carries 39 beats, then selection models that POI as 5 minutes. This is precisely the Rue Cler shape.

### DESIGN C — two-pass select -> plan -> re-budget loop

WHAT CHANGES: keep the dwell-proxy greedy as pass 1; after beat plans exist, compute true voiced seconds per stop; pass 2 corrects the route — if sum(planned) > budget*(1+tol) drop weakest non-endpoint stops (reusing _drop_weakest, selection.py:1625) until within tolerance; if under floor, re-run the fill with the residual TRUE budget. Bounded at 1-2 iterations, monotone (drop-only then fill-only, never oscillate). PROS: greedy internals and all its calibrated constants untouched; lands as a discrete post-pass like endpoint-pull/fill/demotion, individually testable. CONS: (a) the pipeline is already 5 post-passes deep (greedy -> endpoint-pull -> fill -> demotion -> Held-Karp -> vignettes); a 6th that can REMOVE stops after Held-Karp ordering forces a re-order and re-vignette, touching the most fragile seams; (b) demotion/vignette invariants ('pois/transits byte-identical', contract.py:218-222) constrain where it can sit; (c) it still needs planned_audio_seconds anyway, so it is Design A with extra moving parts; (d) dropping a stop pass-2 can evict a pulled endpoint or the fixed-end B sentinel unless specifically guarded — the exact class of bug just fixed in _apply_endpoint_pull (selection.py:1614-1620). TESTS AT RISK: everything A risks, plus ordering/vignette/fixed-end suites. GOLDEN EFFECTS: same direction as A but with a sharper cliff (whole-stop drops instead of earlier budget depletion). Verdict: dominated by A for planning correctness; only preferable if the greedy's constants were untouchable, which they are not.

## Adversarial critique (FIX-FIRST — the corrections are binding)

VERDICT: FIX-FIRST. The inventory is excellent — I independently verified essentially every file:line claim (greedy/fill dwell accounting at selection.py:1058,1061,1370,1422; _sum_audio at generation.py:824-851; dwell fiction at generation.py:808, options.py:107-109/156, trips.py:522, crud/trips.py:45/67/337, audit.py:100-107; dead Route.audio_budget_seconds at contract.py:231/routing.py:250-256; select_poi_beats purity making memoization sound) plus live data (Rue Cler: tier 5, 39 active beats, 19 trigger_addresses, 18 sub_locations, est_spoken_seconds sum 24s, 3,154-word pool — confirmed via dev Neo4j). Designs B and C are analyzed fairly. BUT the recommended Step 3 has an inverted directional analysis at its core: with consumed := max(tier_dwell, planned_audio), honest consumption is pointwise >= the current dwell proxy, so the greedy break fires no later and the fill floor is met no later — Step 3 is monotone stop-count-REDUCING pressure and can never 'make the fill pass try to ADD stops' as claimed. The Ile golden prediction ('unchanged or +1 stop') is mathematically impossible under the design's own floor semantics; the design's abort criterion ('if Ile LOSES a stop, stop and re-design') is the LIKELY outcome, meaning the plan predicts its own failure. Step 3 also creates an unguarded new 1-stop-collapse path (greedy break after one mega-POI on short tours) — the exact failure class this session started from. The PdV safety argument overlooks the demotion coupling (current 66.7% overlap depends on Musée Victor Hugo being selected-then-demoted into PdV). Fix the directional analysis (measure Ile's per-anchor planned audio offline first — cheap with the same memoized function; reconcile Step 3 with the R1 emission relaxations explicitly since they pull goldens in opposite directions), add a greedy-break no-collapse guard, require steps 2+3 in one release, and correct the overstated Step 2 consistency and test-impact claims. Steps 1, 2 (with caveats), and 4 are sound and atomic.

### FATAL DIRECTION ERROR — Step 3 is provably route-shrinking; the 'fill tries to ADD stops' / 'Ile unchanged or +1 stop' claim is impossible under the design's own floor semantics

The recommendation sets consumed-audio := max(compute_dwell_seconds(tier), planned_audio_seconds(poi)). Since max(a,b) >= a, honest per-stop consumption dominates the current dwell proxy POINTWISE. Consequences, verified against the code: (1) the greedy break `consumed_audio >= audio_budget` at selection.py:1061 fires at an earlier-or-equal iteration (the candidate-choice value function at :1039 is audio-free, so the seated sequence is identical up to the earlier break); (2) the fill pass's floor checks at :1371 (`consumed_audio >= floor_audio` -> return early) and :1395 (break) are satisfied earlier-or-equal, so fill adds FEWER-or-equal stops. There is no code path by which honest accounting with a tier floor increases stop count. The designer's Ile rationale ('current script voices well UNDER budget... so honest accounting makes the fill pass try to ADD stops/beats — direction AGREES with the golden aspiration') only makes sense for floor-LESS accounting (where beat-thin stops would consume less than dwell) — i.e., it contradicts the design's own max() floor. Expected real outcome: Ile's tier-5/4 anchors carry flat-capped 8-beat plans (beat_select.py:253-255) whose planned audio plausibly exceeds 300s, so consumed rises, the 2,151s floor is met with fewer than today's 8 stops, visited stops drop, and the 53.2% overlap DROPS — triggering the design's own step-3 abort criterion. The designer measured Rue Cler (3,154-word pool, verified live) and PdV but never measured the Ile anchors' planned audio, despite the entire golden-safety claim riding on it; that measurement is cheap (same memoized planned_audio_seconds run offline) and must precede any implementation. The step-5 deferral compounds this: the diagnostic's R1 relaxation ADDS beats at visited stops (raising planned audio further, shrinking routes more), so 'steps 1-3 before R1' as sequenced pulls the Ile golden in the wrong direction twice.

### Step 3 opens an unguarded 1-stop-collapse path at the greedy break — the same failure class this work started from

Under honest accounting, one beat-rich first anchor can consume the entire audio budget in a single seat: e.g. a 30-min round trip starting at PdV — budget = round(30*0.83*0.6*60) = 896s, PdV planned ~914s -> greedy breaks after 1 stop; the fill pass then sees consumed (914) >= floor (717) at :1371 and returns immediately -> a 1-stop tour with no guard. The no-collapse guard just added covers ONLY _apply_endpoint_pull (the `len(incumbents) <= 1 -> abandon` branch, verified at the ENDPOINT_PULL_MAX_DROPS block); nothing guards the greedy-break path. The design's proposed regression test (c) asserts >=2 stops only for the specific 60-min Rue Cler input (where 817s < 1793s budget, so the break doesn't even fire on the first seat — the test wouldn't exercise this path at all). Whether 1-stop is legitimate here is exactly the design's own human decision 3 (domination policy), but the design neither flags that Step 3 makes the question LIVE for every short-duration request near a mega-POI, nor proposes an invariant/test for it. Required fix: either a greedy-level no-collapse guard (never break with 1 stop while affordable candidates remain) or an explicit product ruling that start-anchor domination on short tours is intended, with a test pinning whichever is chosen.

### PdV golden safety is asserted 'by construction' but has an unverified demotion coupling; the fixture is also mischaracterized as one-stop

The design claims 'PdV's beat plan and hence overlap are unchanged (extras don't penalize)'. Two problems. (1) Demotion coupling: GOLDEN-GAP-DIAGNOSTIC's category-(e) table attributes 2 of PdV's current hits/misses to apply_co_located_demotion merging Musée Victor Hugo's beats into PdV's pool — and trips.py:226 / the golden path merge flavour.demoted_beats into the host plan. Step 3's earlier greedy break can drop the co-located sibling from selection entirely (no seat -> no demotion -> different merged pool -> overlap moves). Likely survivable (a co-located sibling has ~0 insertion cost, so value = base/max(1, extra+1) at selection.py:1039 is huge and it seats early), but 'unchanged by construction' is false as stated — the plan input depends on route composition. Note the golden harness itself (test_tour_golden_pdv.py:118) calls select_poi_beats WITHOUT merging demoted beats, so overlap sensitivity differs between the golden test and the live /trips path — worth pinning before Step 3. (2) Mischaracterization: fixtures/tour_golden/pdv_round_trip_60min.json expected_pois=['Place des Vosges'] is a MUST-INCLUDE list, and fixture note 3 says 'Algorithm naturally pulls neighboring Marais POIs under the spine' — today's PdV route is multi-stop, not the '1-stop 22-beat domination' the summary describes. Under Step 3 (budget 1,793s, PdV ~914s) the sibling count roughly halves; harmless to the overlap metric only IF the demotion coupling holds.

### Steps 2 and 3 are not independently shippable: Step 2 alone broadcasts the planning bug to users on every surface, including mobile

The design sequences 'honesty lands before behavior' and calls Step 2 'zero route change' — true for route shape, but between Step 2 and Step 3 the product visibly violates its duration promise: a 60-min Rue Cler tour's stop card shows ~14 min (options.py:156, trips.py:522), eta_seconds jumps by the same delta (options.py:107-109), and mobile renders all of it — per-stop '${stop.durationMin} min' (mobile/lib/pages/trip_itinerary_page.dart:739), flavour-picker '$etaMin min' (:556,574), saved-trips total (saved_trips_page.dart:109). GeneratedStop.dwell_seconds in the /trips/generate response changes (trips.py:290). options_json itself is safe (trips.py:257 persists only POI ids per flavour). None of this breaks mobile parsing (flutter tests are self-contained JSON fixtures; no timer/auto-advance consumes durationMin — the only Timer is compose-status polling), but the design must state that steps 2+3 ship in the same release, or reorder them, and must add decision 2's pause-allowance question as BLOCKING for step 2 (it changes every displayed number).

### Step 2's 'internally CONSISTENT preview' claim is overstated — the floor and glue seconds keep total_audio_min != sum(stop minutes)

After Step 2, stop minutes = round(max(tier_dwell, planned)/60) while total_audio_min = round(_sum_audio/60) which (a) has no tier floor — a beat-thin tier-5 stop shows 5 min against ~1-2 min of counted narration — and (b) includes 4s per glue sentence and per vignette one-liner (generation.py:850) that belong to no stop's planned audio. The workbench test helper at test_workbench_ui:1921 mocks total_audio_min = sum(s['minutes']) — an identity the UI apparently assumes and which still won't hold after Step 2. The self-contradiction shrinks from ~3x to ~10-20% but is not eliminated; the design should say 'approximately consistent' and decide whether total_audio_min should also gain the floor/pause semantics (decision 2), or the workbench will keep displaying two disagreeing totals. Also note audit.route_eta_seconds (audit.py:106) computes dwell from compute_dwell_seconds(tier) directly, NOT from ScriptPOI.dwell_seconds — Step 2 leaves it on the old fiction, creating a third clock unless it is updated or its test-only status is documented in the step.

### Step 2's test-impact list is wrong in both directions (minor, but it undermines the 'atomic step with updated tests' contract)

test_trip_adapter (:15,:43 — constructs ScriptPOI with hand-set dwell=600 and asserts pass-through), test_trip_models (:212,:262-265), and test_trip_list_api (:89 — dwell_seconds=1800 in a hand-built payload) never exercise generation._flatten_pois; they pin the adapter contract, not tier-derived dwell, so they do NOT need updating for Step 2.
