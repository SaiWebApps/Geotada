## Incident

The workbench's `review.html` reports "Cannot connect to database" whenever its
fetch to the backend fails — which most commonly means nothing is listening on
:8000 at all, because `make api` was never started in this session. The message
text points at Neo4j and repeatedly baited investigations into DB containers,
credentials, and connection code, all healthy. The 5-second diagnosis is
`lsof -iTCP:8000`: empty means start the API, occupied means read the API's own
logs. Related trap: seeing Colima's ssh process bound to :7687 and reading it as
something wrong — that is the normal Docker port-forward for the dev Neo4j.
