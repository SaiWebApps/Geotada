## Deferred items (named by their stop-hook finding id)

### F2 / F3 — judge scheduling is on the serial critical path
A judge consultation measures ~4 min, and a PROVE-FIRST ruling costs another
round-trip. Judge ETAs that budget only for PROCEED are systematically wrong.
Process change to adopt: fire the judge on the DIFF at unit-green, CONCURRENT
with the full bar, and budget one PROVE-FIRST into every judge ETA.
Related: FL-2026-110 (serial critical path).

### F6 — the ledger judge's semantic accuracy is UNPROVEN

    `from .ledger` / `import ledger` anywhere under `src/`);
(b) its semantic accuracy is UNPROVEN — never run live; all 31 tests use a
    hand-labelled offline stub.
Do not let it accrue implied authority from being committed.

### F8 — the findings gate can key on judge prose
Findings F1-F5 and F7 were extracted from judge ruling TEXT by a matcher keying
on the strings `judge-prove-first`, `judge-stop`, `unproven`. A judge explaining
why it did NOT rule STOP therefore manufactures a STOP finding. This does not
void the gate, but it inflates the count with self-referential entries.
Fix direction: have the gate ingest a judge's structured RULING field rather
than grepping its prose.
